Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-28

TwinCity UI keeps the public Cloudflare Pages redirect surface credential-free. Static policy, discovery, and service-offer files do not collect account data.

The live application may process operational events, browser/session diagnostics, and operator actions only for runtime review, dispatch, handoff, and service reliability purposes.

External embeds and analytics stay disabled unless explicitly configured and governed by their provider policies. Do not submit secrets, regulated records, or production incident details through public demo paths.

Privacy requests should use the owner-approved private inquiry lane before any personal or customer operational data is shared.
